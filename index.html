Safety comes first

If a person is in immediate danger in India, call 112. This is India’s official all-emergency number. For a wildlife rescue, contact the relevant State Forest Department or authorized local rescue service.

A bigger picture

One planet.
Countless stories.

WildScope is a public wildlife platform for curious minds—bringing together a growing species guide, field photography, conservation stories and ways to learn.

We’re beginning with India’s remarkable biodiversity and building toward a field guide that can travel across the world.
